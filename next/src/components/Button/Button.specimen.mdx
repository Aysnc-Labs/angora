<Note>
  Keep button labels short — two to three words. Use verbs: "Get started", "Learn more", "Sign up".
</Note>

- **Primary** for the main call-to-action on a page. One per section maximum.
- **Secondary** for supporting actions that complement the primary.
- **Ghost** for tertiary actions, navigation, or when the button should blend with surrounding content.
